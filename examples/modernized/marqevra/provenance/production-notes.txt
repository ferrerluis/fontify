# Marqevra Regular 0.021 · Broadway A · private review

Marqevra is a high-contrast display face inspired by Broadway’s theatrical Art Deco architecture. Broad circular forms, asymmetric dark and light strokes, and curling terminals give its headlines a clear stage presence. Its true lowercase carries that character into mixed-case titles, while coordinated numerals, punctuation, symbols and expanded Latin forms make the design more useful in contemporary editorial and commercial layouts. This Regular build is intended for short headings at 48 px and larger, with a 354-codepoint inventory and tested combining-mark and discretionary-hyphen behavior.
