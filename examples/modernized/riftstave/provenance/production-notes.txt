# Riftstave Regular 0.018 — private ready-to-review build

Riftstave is a pointed brush display face for genre-film headings, cultural posters and expressive commercial headlines. Open angular counters, torn stroke endings and substantial uneven cores carry an urgent handwritten rhythm. It evolves Chiller’s uneasy hand-rendered character through independent generated artwork and explicitly authored extensions. It is not an official Chiller revival.
